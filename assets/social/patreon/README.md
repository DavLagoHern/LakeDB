The two QuerIA launch images follow the established LakeDB post composition:
large logo and white/cyan title on the left, blue/cyan line accents, and a
focused conceptual workflow visual on the right. Patreon announcement art is
campaign artwork, not an application screenshot: it may illustrate the
natural-language-to-SQL transformation, but it must never be described as the
real LakeDB interface.

The QuerIA wordmark must always make its meaning visible: render `Quer` in the
primary text color and the final `IA` in cyan whenever the format permits.
Beta 3.0 capability artwork must distinguish `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`,
`CREATE TABLE` and `ALTER TABLE` at feed size.

Every launch image must communicate one clear idea and use the exact public
version label. The announcement introduces the release; the second image
explains a concrete workflow or capability. Inspect the existing welcome,
release, development and roadmap images before creating a new visual variant.

Real product captures belong in the website and README. Use a native 16:9
Electron capture with no artificial “real capture” title bar, preserve enough
resolution to read the editor, and place a short caption below it explaining
exactly what the image proves.

`tier-community-460x200.png` is available for free-community posts or a future
collection. Patreon handles free membership itself, so it is not configured as
a zero-price paid tier.

## Paid tiers

### LakeDB Supporter

- Price: EUR 5/month
- Free trial: disabled
- Member limit: none
- Shipping address: not required

Description:

> Support the continued development of LakeDB and help fund builds, code
> signing, release infrastructure and the journey toward LakeDB 1.0. LakeDB
> remains the same application for everyone. This membership is a way to
> support the project and follow its development more closely.

Benefits:

- Monthly supporter development update
- Early previews of upcoming features and interface changes
- Supporter roadmap polls
- Behind-the-scenes development notes
- Optional acknowledgement as a LakeDB supporter
- Everything published to free members

### LakeDB Mega Supporter

- Price: EUR 15/month
- Free trial: disabled
- Member limit: none
- Shipping address: not required

Description:

> Give LakeDB a higher level of voluntary support and help cover the ongoing
> cost of builds, signing, distribution and development. Mega Supporters
> receive the same LakeDB application as everyone else and all Supporter
> updates, with optional recognition for their additional contribution.

Benefits:

- Everything included in LakeDB Supporter
- Optional Mega Supporter acknowledgement
- Occasional group roadmap discussion
- A higher level of direct support for independent LakeDB development

The paid tiers must not promise exclusive LakeDB features, priority technical
support or individual meetings. This keeps the membership useful and
sustainable without creating a separate paid edition of the product.

QuerIA launches with a free beta allowance for everyone. Do not describe either
Patreon tier as providing additional QuerIA usage until the application and
service implement that connection and the public terms are updated.
